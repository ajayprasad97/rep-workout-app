Community Guidelines

Effective Date: July 5, 2026  ·  Last Updated: July 5, 2026

Plain-language summary: Rep's social feed is for sharing real training, supporting other lifters, and staying accountable. Be respectful, be honest, and report anything that isn't. These Guidelines work alongside our Terms of Service — if the two ever conflict, the Terms govern.

1. Our Community

Rep's social feed exists to help people train consistently — sharing workouts, cheering on friends, and following other athletes. These Guidelines apply anywhere you interact with others in the app: posts, comments, display names, profile photos, and direct interactions like follows and buddy sessions.

2. Be Respectful

Not allowed, anywhere in the app:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or targeted abuse of another user.
  • Hate speech or content that attacks people based on race, ethnicity, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar characteristics.
  • Sexually explicit content, nudity, or content sexualizing another person without consent.
  • Impersonating another person, brand, or the Rep team.
  • Spam, scams, or repeatedly promoting unrelated products or services.

3. Be Honest

Rep works best when the data behind it is real. Please don't:

  • Fabricate workouts, weights, or personal records to mislead other users.
  • Use bots, scripts, or automated tools to post, like, or follow on your behalf.
  • Create multiple accounts to evade a block, report, or suspension.

Progress looks different for everyone — there's no benefit to inflating your numbers, and it undermines the community for people training honestly.

4. Display Names & Profiles

Your display name and profile photo must be appropriate for a general audience — no profanity, slurs, sexually explicit content, or impersonation. See Terms of Service, Section 5 for how we handle violations.

5. Reporting & Blocking

In the app

You can report or block any user directly from their profile, a post, or the likers list on a post. Blocking is immediate and mutual — a blocked user can no longer see your posts, follow you, or interact with you, and you won't see theirs.

What to report

Report content or accounts that violate these Guidelines — harassment, hate speech, impersonation, spam, or anything that made you feel unsafe. You don't need to block and report at the same time; they're independent actions.

Reporting incorrect exercise data (wrong equipment, weight input, or muscle group) is a separate flow — use the flag icon on an exercise or the ••• menu during a workout. That goes to our exercise-data review queue, not community moderation.

6. Enforcement

We review reports and act proportionally to the violation:

  • Minor or first-time issues (e.g. an inappropriate display name): a warning and a chance to fix it, typically within 7 days.
  • Repeated or moderate issues: temporary restriction of posting or social features.
  • Severe issues (threats, hate speech, sexual content involving minors, illegal activity): immediate and permanent account suspension, without prior warning.

This mirrors the moderation process described in Terms of Service, Section 5.

7. Appeals

Think we got it wrong?

Email us and we'll take a second look. We'll respond within 30 days.
