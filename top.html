Top 10 Most Popular Exercises

Ranked by usage across the Rep community — from compound staples to isolation work that never goes out of style.

Updated September 14, 2026

#1 Most Popular
Pec Deck

Sit with your back flat against the pad, forearms on the arms of the machine, and bring them together in front of your chest in a controlled arc. An isolation fly that hits the chest hard without any shoulder stabilization demands.

Chest Machine Beginner
#2 Most Popular
Barbell Bench Press

Unrack the barbell, lower it to your chest under control, then press back to lockout. The foundational upper-body push movement for chest, shoulder, and triceps strength — a gym staple for good reason.

Chest Triceps · Shoulders Barbell Beginner
#3 Most Popular
Lat Pulldown

Grip the bar wide, lean back slightly, and pull it down to your upper chest while driving your elbows toward your hips. A cable-based vertical pull that builds lat width and back strength without needing a pull-up bar.

Back Biceps Cable Beginner
#4 Most Popular
Leg Press

Seat yourself in the leg press machine and push the sled away by extending your legs, keeping feet shoulder-width apart. A compound quad and glute builder that reduces spinal loading compared to squats.

Quadriceps Glutes Machine Beginner
#5 Most Popular
Cable Tricep Pushdown

Stand at a cable station, elbows pinned to your sides, and press the bar or rope down to full extension before controlling it back up. A staple triceps isolation move that keeps constant tension through the whole rep.

Triceps Cable Beginner
#6 Most Popular
Leg Extensions

Sit in the leg extension machine and extend your legs to full lockout under control. An isolation exercise that directly targets the quadriceps without axial load — ideal for finishing a leg session or knee rehab.

Quadriceps Machine Beginner
#7 Most Popular
Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Press dumbbells from shoulder height to full extension overhead, keeping your core braced throughout. Builds pressing strength and shoulder width through a natural arc of motion that spares the joints.

Shoulders Triceps Dumbbell Intermediate
#8 Most Popular
Hammerstrength Incline Chest Press

Set the seat so the handles sit at upper-chest height, then press each side independently through a fixed incline path. A plate-loaded machine press that targets the upper chest with less stabilization demand than a barbell.

Upper Chest Shoulders · Triceps Machine Intermediate
#9 Most Popular
Machine Hip Abductor

Sit in the machine with the pads against the outside of your knees, then push your legs apart against the resistance. An isolation move that strengthens the glute medius and outer hip for better stability in squats, lunges, and single-leg work.

Abductors Machine Beginner
#10 Most Popular
Machine Hip Adductor

Sit in the machine with the pads against the outside of your knees, then squeeze your legs together against the resistance. An isolation move that strengthens the often-neglected adductors for hip stability and injury resilience.
